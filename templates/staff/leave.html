Where do Leave Requests come from?

Leave and absence requests in KidSynq originate from two standard channels:

1. Educator & Staff Self-Service: Teachers and educators submit vacation or sick leave requests directly from their Staff Portal (/login/?type=staff) or mobile kiosk. Their requests arrive here with status Pending for director approval.
2. Daycare Admin Direct Entry: Center directors can log an absence, planned vacation, or medical emergency directly on behalf of any staff member using the "+ Record Staff Leave" button above.
